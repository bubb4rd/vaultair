Security health keeps a history

A snapshot is recorded each time you unlock, so the dashboard shows how open issues change instead of just today's count.

  • One dot is one open issue. Hover a column for the day and count.
  • History starts today. Nothing earlier is backfilled, so the plot fills in as you unlock.
  • Stored in your vault. Snapshots are encrypted with everything else and never leave the device.

B · Release spotlight

When it shows

  • Once, on the first unlock after an update that carries a spotlight. Never on a patch release and never mid-task: only on the dashboard.
  • 800×460 dialog on the popover surface, 12 px overlay radius, hairline strong border, over a 72% backdrop. Esc or the close button dismisses it.
  • Dismissed early? The sidebar keeps a one-row "Updated to v1.4.0" entry above Settings until it is opened or closed with its x.

Anatomy

  • Left: the clip pane on the page background. A 360×260 stage with the feature drawn at real proportions, a "Playing" status pill, and a scrub bar with mono times. Clips are 5 to 10 s, muted, loop.
  • Right: version in mono with an "Up to date" status (icon + text + secure green), a 20 px title, one sentence, then up to three check-bulleted facts with the lead in foreground and the rest muted.
  • Bottom: the onboarding progress pattern. One segment per spotlight (max 3), the current one filling in brand blue, stage name under it, "1 / 3" in mono. Next is the one filled button and names the next stage.

Behaviour

  • Next advances with the onboarding slide (300 ms, 24 px), reduced motion swaps instantly and shows the clip's final frame as a still.
  • The last step's primary becomes "Done"; "All changes" opens the full list (variation A's popover, or a Settings › Updates page).
  • Assets ship in the build as .webm + a .webp poster; the pane shows the poster until the clip can play.
  • Spotlight count and copy live in updates.json; a release with no spotlight shows nothing.
